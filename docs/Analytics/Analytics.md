#### Introduction to Analytics

The system is based on deep learning technology, supports Intel CPU and NVIDIA GPU inference, supports real-time analysis of perimeter and illegal behavior, and stores metadata in a streaming video database that can be searched based on different conditions, providing more powerful security prevention strategies and post-event investigation methods.The platform detects abnormal behavior and suspicious activities based on user-defined rules, providing real-time alert reporting without the need for continuous manual monitoring.Realize 7×24 hours of uninterrupted reasoning, greatly reduce the workload of manual processing of massive video data, and proactively identify potential safety hazards and prevent suspicious risks.
